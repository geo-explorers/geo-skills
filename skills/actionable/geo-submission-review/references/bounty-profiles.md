# Bounty review paths

Always read the complete bounty and its applicable linked criteria. Record specification snapshot/effective date and the item-level review unit. The following routes organize checks; they do not add requirements to the bounty.

| Path | Review focus |
|---|---|
| Bespoke dataset/page/deliverable | Required coverage, columns/relations, thresholds and evidence. Inventory the submitted IDs across constituent proposals and required spaces. Test completeness against full requirements; a named required item omitted from a description may still be explicitly permitted. Numeric counts need verified definitions: link presence or keyword proxies do not establish an audit determination, source quality or an affected component. Use dataset-validator and ontology-advisor where needed. |
| News | Read news-review.md. Distinct substantial event, direct relevance/impact for the target space, duplicate event identity and first-submitted precedence. Generated copy remains editable by curators: verified content errors go to curator feedback; backend failures go to technical QA. Selection, copy readiness and governance/points are separate. |
| X posts | Read the complete post, parent, quote and relevant thread. Apply actual significance/relevance, originality and attribution requirements. Opinion/analysis can qualify; do not transfer the news-only new-event rule. An author's background or imported topic does not prove the post's relevance. Authors, Related people and Topics are different relations. |
| Blogs | Read full article and its attribution, date and underlying sources. Check the bounty's substantive analysis/relevance/originality criteria. Distinguish reporting, author opinion and factual claims. |
| Fact-checks | Verify the exact submitted correction, scope/network/version, original and proposed values against primary evidence. Count objective corrected facts by the bounty's unit; coupled address/explorer edits may be one correction. Preserve rejection of an older bundle separately from acceptance of a valid revision. Claims/arguments need source traceability and ontology checks where required. |
| Organize claims | Review the scoped claim inventory, source fidelity, typed links, supporting/opposing relationships and grouping meaning. Use geo-claim-grouping's applicable process. Do not infer contradiction from wording alone or treat a graph-link count as semantic correctness. |

A case with unknown type, unclear item allocation, unavailable full requirements or incomplete sources stays unresolved with its exact next action. Missing evidence is a contributor defect only when the actual requirement makes supplying it their responsibility.
